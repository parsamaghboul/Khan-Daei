Booking Rules:

Title Description
Reservation Reservations are available for the next day only. Full table payment is required at the time of booking.
Cancellation Cancellations must be made at least 3 hours prior to your reservation time for a full refund.
Confirmation Our team will contact you within 1 hour of receiving your request to confirm your reservation.
Holding Policy Tables will be held for up to 15 minutes past your reservation time before being released.
Pet Policy To ensure the comfort of all guests, pets are strictly not allowed inside the restaurant.
Dress Code Guests are kindly requested to maintain appropriate and formal attire in accordance with local regulations.
Seating Preference Table placement (Indoor or Outdoor) is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed during peak hours.
Reservation Changes Any changes to the reservation details must be requested in advance and are subject to availability.
Outside Food Outside food and beverages are not permitted inside the restaurant.
Guest Limit Reservations are valid only for the number of guests specified at the time of booking.
